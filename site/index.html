What the hackathon asks us to solve

Build an AI-enabled Antarctic sea-ice, iceberg trajectory, and navigation decision-support system that combines satellite imagery, weather/ocean data, forecasting, and risk-aware route optimization.

  • Iceberg tracks with 6h, 12h, 24h, and 3-day forecasts
  • Risk map over a route graph with safe-path recommendations
